What the license covers

The rights

One licensee per market, held for the term. You sell it, you brand it, you keep the client relationships. The format stays ours; the edition is yours.

  • Exclusive in your market for the term
  • Your host and your branding on screen
  • License fee plus production billed per show — no share of your sales
  • Multi-year term with an annual minimum
  • Local broadcast airtime available separately

The build

We design the hosting side of your show with you, and nothing goes into production until you have approved how it looks and who is in front of the camera. From there you can stay in the creative on every episode or hand it over entirely — your call, and it can change client to client.

  • Host designed to your market, in any language
  • Hosted in any language you need, including dual-language editions
  • Set designed to your brand
  • Full graphics and titles package
  • Optional: a branded YouTube channel built for the show
  • Production method and standards
  • An upload portal for your team
  • As much or as little creative involvement as you want
